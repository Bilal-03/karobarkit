# Everyday business simplification

Audience: Indian small business owners and freelancers. The direction is an owner-selected hypothesis, not a customer research finding.

## Delivered behavior

Home leads with invoice, quotation, receipt, UPI QR, pricing and GST. Primary navigation is Home, Documents, Saved drafts and More tools. The directory keeps six task groups and search; legacy category/type/execution/scope URLs still filter, with an applied-filter notice and Clear action. Documents are ordered quotation, invoice, GST invoice, receipt, then other generators. The scenario workbench is a secondary advanced link. /get-started redirects home and is removed from the sitemap.

Product Studio remains available from the case study, outside customer workspace links. Existing comparisons and studio records remain in the version 1 workspace and its full backup/delete operations. No migration discards them.

Drafts load automatically on /workspace. Continue editing passes only an opaque draft ID. Supported forms read and validate the stored structure, load incomplete drafts, and run normal required-field/calculation validation for export. Save changes updates the same ID; Save a copy creates a new ID. Concurrent changes to that record prevent overwrite. Business details apply only on request. Save controls sit below the form; a small saved-details shortcut stays near the top. Nothing autosaves. Logos remain excluded. Invalid/missing local data, quota failures and stale drafts have visible recovery messages. Backups use the existing strict version 1 format, including legacy fields.

Quotation, ordinary invoice, GST invoice, receipt and UPI start without sample identities. Document dates use the local current date. Example previews are explicit and cannot be saved, printed, exported or handed off through the application. Start my own document clears sample inputs. Normal document exports require a successful review of the current inputs. Editing afterwards disables export until reviewed again. GST required fields and arithmetic engines are preserved.

Document steps use Business & customer, Details & items (Payment details for receipts), Review & download. Mobile preview appears on review rather than through an empty toggle. Due/validity dates are not appearance options. Payment & terms, Contact details and Appearance group optional settings. Receipt footer appears under Appearance in the details step. Download PDF is primary; print and next steps are secondary.

Pricing starts with zero optional discount/tax, exposes applied assumptions even when controls are collapsed, and labels its result Calculated selling price. Examples still demonstrate nonzero adjustments. Cost, margin basis, discount, tax rate and amount are included in the complete result. Monetary breakdowns are not limited to three rows. No calculation formulas were changed.

## Evidence and limits

Before/after homepage images appear in /case-study. Browser checks use verification traffic and simulated inputs only. Vercel custom events stay disabled. No adoption, customer interviews, conversion improvement or commercial outcomes are claimed. Local browser storage is not cloud sync or a permanent accounting archive.

Deferred: accounts, cloud sync, payment tracking, automated reminders, CRM, inventory, more AI and more calculators.
