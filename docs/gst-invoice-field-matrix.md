# GST invoice field-requirement matrix

Reviewed 6 August 2026 against official CBIC material only. This matrix describes the narrow standard tax-invoice draft workflow in Milestone 5; it is not a legal conclusion for every taxpayer or transaction.

## Deliberate non-claims

The generator does not decide whether a party is registered, whether a GSTIN exists, which HSN/SAC or rate applies, whether a supply is exempt, whether reverse charge applies, whether CGST or UTGST is appropriate, whether ITC is available, whether registration or filing is required, or whether e-invoicing applies. Those require transaction facts, taxpayer status, reviewed policy and sometimes official portal or professional review.
