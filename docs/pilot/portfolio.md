Use [the current portfolio guide](../portfolio-guide.md) for resume wording now. Interviews and customer metrics below are not required and must not be claimed as completed.

Target product internships/APM, product operations and growth associate roles; use basic SQL and spreadsheet analysis to support decisions.

## Current honest project summary

Built and shipped a browser-based business toolkit, introduced a freelancer paperwork workflow, and prepared privacy-safe product measurement and a research pilot. External adoption and commercial demand have not yet been validated.

## Case study to complete after the pilot

1. Customer/problem: who was interviewed, how recruited, recent task and current workaround.
2. Evidence: observations versus interpretations, contradictions and sample limitations.
3. Decision: selected problem, alternatives rejected, feasibility and cost tradeoffs.
4. Intervention: workflow before/after, short PRD and acceptance results.
5. Outcomes: [a/b] baseline and [c/d] retest completions, time/sample sizes, source and period. Include failures and assisted outcomes. Leave missing measures unreported.
6. Next decision: what to keep, change or stop; what evidence would change your mind.

Do not publish participant details or quotes without permission. Do not call targets, internal tests or hypothetical metrics achieved traction.

## Three-minute demo script

0:00–0:30 customer, current workaround and unresolved hypothesis.
0:30–1:30 show quotation → invoice → receipt with dummy data, explicit imports and export.
1:30–2:15 show measured funnel/research findings once available; until then label measurement setup and pending evidence.
2:15–3:00 explain one prioritization choice, one failed assumption and next experiment. If no failed assumption has yet been observed, say so.

## Resume bullet after evidence exists

Interviewed [N] freelancers, identified [problem], and prioritized [workflow improvement]; observed successful task completion change from [a/b] to [c/d] during a [duration] pilot. Report the small sample and measurement method in the linked case study; do not attribute causality without an appropriate design.

## Interview preparation

Be ready to explain: Why freelancers? What evidence contradicted you? Why no mandatory login? How do you distinguish an export from customer value? Which metric could mislead you? What did AI do versus what you decided? What did you choose not to build? What would make you stop this project?
