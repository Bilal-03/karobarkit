# Phase 2 — recruitment and discovery

Status: recruitment setup prepared; zero participants recruited or interviewed in this record. Target: 5–8 interviews. The recruitment page is /research; it uses the existing contact email delivery. No invitations have been sent by the assistant.

## Recruitment sequence

1. Make a private list of people you know who freelance in India, plus communities where moderator-approved research posts are permitted. Do not scrape contacts.
2. Use the outreach/community drafts in growth.md. Link to /research?kk_channel=outreach or /research?kk_channel=community. Analytics remains disabled on Hobby, so record recruitment source manually.
3. Ask whether they prepared a quotation/invoice in the last three months. Do not require them to use KarobarKit first. Aim for different freelance services and levels of experience; explicitly record convenience-sample bias.
4. Reply to volunteers to confirm eligibility and mutually agree a 20-minute time. Keep scheduling contacts out of the repository and anonymous research log. Decline ineligible volunteers politely without retaining unnecessary information.
5. Before starting, obtain permission for anonymous notes using research.md. Recording, public quotes and follow-up each require separate permission. Interest-form contact consent alone is insufficient.
6. Interview about their current process before showing the product. Observe dummy-data tasks without coaching. Record assisted and failed attempts as well as successful ones.
7. After each interview, complete interview-record.md; after 5–8, complete discovery-summary.md. Do not choose the next feature solely from the loudest suggestion.

## Phase exit criteria

- 5–8 completed interviews, or an explicit documented recruitment limitation.
- Recent-task/current-workaround evidence, frequency, consequences and observed failures.
- Clear separation of observations, interpretations and assumptions.
- One repeated consequential problem selected with counter-evidence and alternative explanations recorded.
- No claimed demand, traction or improvement based solely on invitations, interest submissions or internal tests.
