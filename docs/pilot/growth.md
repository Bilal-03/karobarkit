# Acquisition experiments — drafts, not sent

Hypothesis: targeted, task-specific invitations produce more activated freelancers per hour than broad promotion. Do not pay for acquisition during the initial pilot. Record invitations/eligible respondents/activated participants, outreach minutes, spending and feedback separately by channel. Deduplicate recruited participants manually with their consent; never join their identities to product analytics.

## Personalized outreach draft

Hi [name], I’m testing a free tool for Indian freelancers to prepare quotations, invoices and receipts without an account. Have you handled this paperwork recently? I’d value 20 minutes to understand your current process and watch you try a task with dummy details. No client records needed, and you can stop anytime. Interested?

Link after agreement: https://karobarkit.vercel.app/freelancers?kk_channel=outreach

## Community draft — obtain moderator permission first

I’m researching how Indian freelancers handle quotations and invoices. I’ve built KarobarKit and am looking for a small number of people who recently prepared client paperwork to try a short task and share what is confusing. Free, no account, dummy details only. This is an early pilot; usefulness has not yet been validated. Please reply privately if interested.

Link: https://karobarkit.vercel.app/freelancers?kk_channel=community

Run both for one week. Review actual counts and effort; small nonrandom samples do not prove channel superiority. If neither recruits participants, revise the message or audience access before adding features. Do not claim that page views equal customers or activation equals payment.

Ask about saved details/templates only after observing repeat friction. Record stated interest, past spending, any actual commitment and actual payments as different evidence levels. Review hosting and AI bills separately each week; money spent and owner time are separate acquisition costs.
