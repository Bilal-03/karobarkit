# Discovery and task-test kit

## Recruitment and consent

Target Indian freelancers who have prepared a quotation or invoice recently. Aim for varied services and experience; record how recruitment may bias findings. Ask permission for a 20-minute conversation and anonymous notes. Recording is optional and requires separate explicit permission. Participation is voluntary; participants can stop or ask for deletion. Do not request real client documents or financial records. Use dummy information during observed tasks.

Assign P01, P02, etc. Keep contact/consent records outside this public repository. Default to deleting contact details and identifiable notes 30 days after the pilot; retain only anonymized findings with consent. Obtain separate permission for public quotations or follow-up contact.

## Interview script

1. Tell me about the last time you quoted or invoiced a client. Walk me through what happened.
2. What did you use? What did you copy, calculate or retype?
3. Where did you hesitate, make a mistake or ask for help? What happened next?
4. How often does this happen? What is the consequence of getting it wrong?
5. What alternatives have you tried? Why do you keep your current approach?
6. What happens after the client accepts, and after payment arrives?
7. If details/templates could be reused, when would that help? Have you paid for a workaround? Stated interest is not willingness-to-pay validation.

Show KarobarKit only after understanding their current process. Avoid asking whether they like your idea or would hypothetically use it.

## Baseline and retest tasks

Use the same dummy service and client details. Ask participants to prepare a quotation, turn accepted work into an invoice, then create a receipt after a simulated payment. Observe without coaching. Record task start, first successful export, assistance, errors, abandoned steps and outcome (success/assisted/failure). Capture interruptions separately from task time. Where feasible, vary the order of old/new flows to reduce learning effects; report that the small sample does not establish causality.

Tax comprehension task: show ₹18,00,000 taxable income, ₹1,60,000 tax and ₹6,400 cess. Ask participants to explain ₹1,66,400 total in their own words. Record accuracy and hesitation; do not tell them the answer first.

Follow up on the next relevant task, typically 2–4 weeks: Did the document help with a real job? Did they use it again? Why or why not? Ask only participants who agreed to follow-up.
